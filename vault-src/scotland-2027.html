Weather & what to pack

Late April into early May is spring at its kindest in Scotland — statistically among the driest, sunniest weeks of the year, and blessed with ~16 hours of daylight (light past 9pm). Expect mild, changeable days: mid-50s°F by afternoon, low-40s°F overnight, a passing shower as likely as sun. The figures below are long-term May climate normals for each stop.

StopAvg highAvg lowRain (May)Sun / day
Edinburgh13°C · 55°F6°C · 42°F~50 mm · 10 days~6.5 hrs
Inverness · Highlands13°C · 55°F5°C · 40°F~55 mm · 11 days~6 hrs
Glasgow15°C · 59°F7°C · 45°F~70 mm · 12 days~6.3 hrs

Normals describe the shape of the week, not a forecast — check the real one ~10 days out. Glasgow's west-coast position makes it the wettest of the three, though May is still its driest month. Pack layers, a waterproof shell, and broken-in walking shoes; the Highlands (Glen Coe, Loch Ness) run a few degrees cooler and windier than the cities, so a warm layer earns its place on the boat cruise and the evening sessions.

The crossover is somewhere past four hours. At half a day Viator actually undercuts the STGA card for two people — the earlier note in this dossier said otherwise, and at 4 hours it was wrong. Rosslyn on Day 3 — the Sunday problem, then who to book

Day 3 is Sunday 2 May 2027, and Rosslyn Chapel doesn't open until 12:00 on Sundays (9:00–17:00 Mon–Sat). A morning Rosslyn half-day that day is not possible. It has to be the afternoon — which happens to fit the STGA rule that a half day either finishes by 13:00 or starts from 13:30. Book the guide from 13:30.

Two more things about the chapel that shape the booking: it runs on 90-minute timed slots and you should book the slot in advance (£12 adult), and admission already includes a short talk by one of the chapel's own expert guides, given half an hour into each slot. Your Blue Badge guide gives you the Borders context, the drive, and the carvings-and-legends framing; the chapel's staff cover the interior. Don't pay a private guide for eight hours expecting them to lecture inside — that's not what the visit is.

Finding a guide with genuine Rosslyn experience is harder than it should be. STGA's directory has no Rosslyn filter — 159 Edinburgh Blue Badge guides match English-language walking tours, the profiles list structured specialisms rather than itineraries, and not one of the twelve who tag "Religion" mentions Rosslyn anywhere. So it can't be filtered for. It has to be asked for, or sourced from the guides who advertise it. Four leads, verified:
