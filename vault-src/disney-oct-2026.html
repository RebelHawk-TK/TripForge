01

The shape of it

One base, no hotel changes: you check into Disney's Riviera Resort on Sunday and stay put for all four nights. Epcot bookends the trip — a park day on either end — with a round of golf in the middle and a day off-property at Universal's brand-new Epic Universe. The Riviera sits on the Skyliner, so Epcot is a gondola ride away with no car and no parking.
